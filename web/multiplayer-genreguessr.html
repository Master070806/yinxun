How to play

Each round has two stages: everyone selects a cluster, then players who chose correctly select a genre. A wrong cluster earns 0. In the second stage, score = 1,000 × (1 − distance / farthest distance), using the chosen metric and the farthest genre in the correct cluster. The target earns 1,000. Everyone readies up between rounds; the highest total wins.
