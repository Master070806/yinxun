Cartography & BGMs

About

Cartography & BGMs is a collection of games about listening and exploring musical genres. An invitation to find your way through familiar sounds and unfamiliar names.

In Genreguessr, listen to an anonymous sample and choose its cluster. A correct choice unlocks a second sample from the same genre; then choose the exact genre. Shape the neighborhoods with one of three metrics—XYRGB, graph distance, or signed kernel—and adjust its parameters before you begin.

Genre names, colors, connections, and music samples come from Every Noise at Once, created by Glenn McDonald.
